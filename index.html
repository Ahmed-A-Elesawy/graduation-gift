This Website Isn't Down

And it's not a problem with the internet.

I simply deactivated it.

🎀​

I think I owe you an explanation for that.

I put a lot of time and thought into making this for you. It wasn't just a website; I collected little pieces of memories, old photos, the things you used to send me, the flowers, the exhibitions and competitions we went to, and all those small moments that meant something to you. I wanted it to be something you could look back at and smile.

When I finally sent it to you, I think a part of me was waiting for something very simple — not praise, not a big thank-you, and definitely not anything you owed me. I just wanted to feel that you were happy to see it, that you liked it, or that it meant something to you.

And when I didn't really feel that, I won't lie, it made me feel bad a little. Maybe you didn't mean anything by it, and maybe you had your own reasons at the time, but from my side, I couldn't help wondering whether I had done too much, or whether I had crossed a line somewhere without realizing it.

I think I felt something similar with the little yellow note I made for you at Eid Al-Adha. I never needed a huge reaction. I just wanted to know that the little things I did actually reached you.

So I decided to deactivate the website. Not because I regret making it, and not because I want you to feel guilty about it. I actually don't regret it at all. I'm still glad I got to make something so personal, special and creative for you, and I'm glad those memories exist somewhere, even if things between us eventually took a different path.

I don't know if you ever opened it again after that day, or if you even got to see everything properly. But I genuinely hope that, at least for one moment, it made you smile.

Maybe that's enough for me now — to know that I made something special for you once, with nothing but good intentions and love behind it.

So this isn't really a website that stopped working. It's just a little chapter I decided to put on pause for now.

Maybe it'll stay that way for a while, maybe longer than that. But nothing has been erased. The memories, the pictures, everything is still there — just quietly paused.

And maybe that's okay.

With all my heart,

and still proud of you.
